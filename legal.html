Legal

Privacy notice, data handling, and third-party acknowledgements for this website.

Summary

This website sets no cookies and stores no persistent identifier on your device. It provides no account system, no comment facility, and no forms, and does not request your name, email address, or any other personal detail. As no information is stored on or read from your device, no consent banner is required under the ePrivacy Directive as implemented in Ireland (S.I. No. 336/2011).

Analytics

This website uses Google Analytics 4 in cookieless mode. Consent for analytics_storage and ad_storage is set to denied by default, before the measurement tag is initialised. In this configuration Google Analytics transmits anonymous events rather than writing a _ga cookie, and page views cannot be linked into a profile of an individual visitor across sessions.

The following is transmitted with each event: IP address (used to derive an approximate country or region, and not retained thereafter), browser and device type, the page requested, and the referring page. An IP address constitutes personal data under the General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), which is the basis for this notice.

Third-party recipients

Where a page loads a file hosted by another organisation, your browser issues a request directly to that organisation's server, which necessarily discloses your IP address to it. This is inherent to how the web operates and is not a transfer of any further information. The organisations concerned are:

  • Google — analytics (googletagmanager.com) and web fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com)
  • ArtStation, operated by Epic Games — delivery of artwork images
  • GitHub — profile image, repository statistics, and stored statistics data
  • Lanyard (api.lanyard.rest) — Discord presence information
  • ghchart (ghchart.rshah.org) — contribution activity chart
  • jsDelivr, Iconify, Icons8, Wikimedia Foundation, Fandom, and Adobe — interface icons
  • api.mista.tech — a server operated by the site operator, providing live project statistics

None of the above are advertising networks. None receives any information beyond that which a browser transmits with an ordinary request for a file.

Rights of the data subject

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR you have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of personal data concerning you, to request restriction of processing, and to object to processing carried out on the basis of legitimate interest.

A practical limitation should be stated clearly. Because analytics are operated without cookies or any other persistent identifier, no data held can be attributed to an identified or identifiable individual, and the records cannot be searched by reference to a person. In the terms of Article 11 of the GDPR, the controller is not in a position to identify the data subject. Requests will nonetheless be answered, and will set out what data exists.

Complaints may be addressed to the Data Protection Commission, Ireland (dataprotection.ie), or to the supervisory authority of your place of residence.
